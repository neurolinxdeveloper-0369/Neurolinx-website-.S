# 01 — Home Screen & Master Navigation

## 1. Master Navigation (Inverted Ribbon Header)

Based on the design update, the global header utilizes an "inverted ribbon" architecture to organize complex e-commerce and corporate navigation while maintaining a modern, professional aesthetic.

### Visual Architecture
- **Top Bar (Pure Light `#FFFFFF`)**: Contains the utility functions. It spans the full width and houses the Global Search bar, Contact Us button, Login, and Global Delivery info.
- **Bottom Bar (Industrial Charcoal `#4B4C4C`)**: Acts as the primary navigation ribbon. It houses the core R&D and commercial product categories (Automation, AI, Robotics, Semiconductor Fab, etc.).
- **Centerpiece Integration**: A central circular cutout element overlaps both the white and charcoal bars. This central node houses the Neurolinx brand mark/icon, serving as the anchor point of the entire navigation structure.

## 2. Hero Section (First Viewport)
- **Background**: Full-bleed edge-to-edge plane (optimized 3D or cinematic video) representing Smart Tech Solutions.
- **Content**: 
  - Headline: "Smart Tech Solutions in Automation, AI, Robotics & Energy"
  - Primary CTA: "Explore Technologies"
  - Accent Ribbons: Top/Left highlight ribbons in `#C81623` on hero overlay panels.

## 3. Post-Hero Structure
Follows the progression from R&D capability down to commercial e-commerce products:
1. **Capability Promise**: Engineering excellence statement.
2. **Featured Solutions / R&D**: Highlighting AI, Chip Design, and Automation.
3. **Commercial Products Showcase**: E-commerce bridge for LED, BLDC, Power Supplies.
4. **Global Sectors**: Railways, Wind/Solar infrastructure.
